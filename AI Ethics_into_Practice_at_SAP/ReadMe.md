### **01: Why Responsible AI Requires high Ethical, Security, and Privacy Standards**
- AI impacts various business areas like **finance, HR, supply chain, and IT**.
- **SAP Business AI must be**:
  - **Relevant** → AI should drive business impact.
  - **Reliable** → AI should be based on **accurate business data**.
  - **Responsible** → AI should **meet ethical, security, and privacy standards**.
- SAP follows **ethical principles and regulatory frameworks** like:
  - **UNESCO’s AI ethics recommendation**
  - **EU AI Act**
  - **OECD guidelines**
- **Technical robustness** is key to responsible AI development.

---

### **02: SAP’s Guiding Principles for AI Ethics & Global AI Ethics Policy**
SAP’s **AI ethics policy** ensures **ethical AI development, deployment, and use**.

#### **Key guiding principles**:
1. **Do-No-Harm** → AI must respect **human rights**.
2. **Safety & Security** → AI must comply with **SAP’s global security policies**.
3. **Fairness & Non-Discrimination** → AI should **eliminate biases**.
4. **Sustainability** → AI should support **social, economic, and environmental sustainability**.
5. **Privacy & Data Protection** → AI must comply with **GDPR & SAP policies**.
6. **Human Oversight** → AI must be monitored using approaches like **"human-in-the-loop"**.
7. **Transparency & Explainability** → AI’s **decisions must be clear and accountable**.

---

### **03: SAP’s Internal AI Governance Framework**
SAP has a **multi-layered governance** structure for AI ethics:
1. **Executive Board** → Approves AI ethics policies.
2. **Global AI Ethics Steering Committee** → Oversees AI strategy.
3. **AI Ethics Office** → Monitors AI ethics compliance.
4. **Corporate Sustainability Team** → Ensures **independent oversight**.
5. **External AI Ethics Advisory Panel** → Provides **expert review**.

#### **AI risk assessment process**:
- AI use cases go through an **impact assessment**.
- **Red Line Cases** → Prohibited AI use (e.g., surveillance violating human rights).
- **High-Risk AI** → Requires **Ethics Committee review**.

### **04: High-Risk AI Use Cases That Were Stopped**
#### **Two high-risk AI projects were stopped due to ethical concerns**:
1. **AI Mental Health Support System**
   - Proposed AI **analyzing employee text** for signs of mental distress.
   - **Issues**: Possible **misdiagnosis, cultural bias, and legal risks**.
   - **Decision**: **Stopped** due to **high ethical risk**.
   
2. **AI Presentation Feedback System**
   - AI analyzing **speech, tone, and presentation style**.
   - **Issues**: **Bias in voice recognition, gender & accent discrimination**.
   - **Decision**: **Stopped** due to **risk of workplace discrimination**.

---

### **05: Ensuring Transparency & Explainability in HR AI**
- SAP **Fieldglass Resume Assessment System**:
  - AI helps **rank candidates** based on job descriptions.
  - **AI Ethics Concerns**:
    - Could **affect hiring decisions unfairly**.
    - Must be **transparent & explainable**.
  - **Solutions Implemented**:
    - AI **disclaimer & documentation** for transparency.
    - **Explainability** → AI provides **detailed breakdown** of why a candidate was recommended.

---

### **06: Ensuring Fairness in HR AI (Bias Mitigation)**
- The **resume assessment AI** was tested for **biases**.
- SAP used **counterfactual fairness testing**:
  - AI **evaluated identical resumes** but with **different demographic attributes**.
  - Results showed **gender biases** in hiring recommendations.

#### **Bias Mitigation Actions**:
- **Removed ranking system** → Switched to a **fit-based evaluation**.
- **Focused only on skills** to eliminate demographic biases.

#### **Outcome**:
AI now makes **fairer hiring recommendations**.

### **07: Managing Standard AI Use Cases (SAP AI Governance)**
- **AI-driven visual inspection** in SAP Digital Manufacturing.
- This **AI automates defect detection** in manufacturing.

#### **Risk classification**:
- Not a **Red Line case**.
- Classified as **low risk** but still requires monitoring.

#### **Key Features Implemented**:
- **Human oversight** → Users can **override AI decisions**.
- **Explainability** → AI clearly **labels defects** for better decision-making.

## **08: Integrating AI Ethics into the AI Development Lifecycle**
SAP ensures AI ethics at **all development stages**:
1. **Ideation** → Define the AI use case.
2. **Validation** → Assess **feasibility & risks**.
3. **Realization** → Develop AI functions.
4. **Productization** → Integrate AI into products.
5. **Operations** → Monitor & improve AI systems.

#### **Key AI roles at SAP**:
- **AI Use Case Owner** → Oversees AI development.
- **AI Data Scientist** → Develops AI models.
- **AI Engineer** → Implements AI in products.
- **AI Ethics Team** → Ensures AI follows ethics policies.

SAP provides the **AI Ethics Handbook** to guide employees on responsible AI.

---

### **Key Takeaways**
  - **SAP follows strict AI ethics policies** to ensure **fairness, transparency, and security**.  
  - **High-risk AI projects are reviewed & stopped if necessary** (e.g., mental health AI, biased hiring AI).  
  - **SAP's AI governance model ensures compliance** with regulations like **GDPR & UNESCO AI Ethics Framework**.  
  - **AI fairness & explainability are key priorities**, especially in **HR and hiring AI**.
